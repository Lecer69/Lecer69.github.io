Dumble Land

An Iranian Minecraft server where a lot of the gameplay runs on our own code. Together with The_Dumbledodo I've written most of its plugins: gameplay, the economy, the proxy network and the Discord side. The repos are private, so here's what each one does.

Built on Paper, Velocity, Blueprint, packetevents, Slimefun, JDA and Gradle

  1. DumbolRolePlay

    A whole roleplay gamemode: jobs and professions, gangs, bounties and wanted levels, robberies and prison, ATMs, taxis, medkits and revives, custom items and loot boxes.

  2. DumbolCurrency

    The server's currencies and store, shared across the network through Velocity. Players buy ranks, items, Slimefun gear and perks, every purchase is logged to Discord, and big ones start a GG wave that rewards everyone who says gg.

  3. DumbolExpansion

    Our own Slimefun addon: generators, quarries, excavators, capacitors, storage and research, with dozens of custom items and machines.

  4. DumbolNetwork

    The Velocity proxy plugin and its Paper bridge: the layer that connects every server on the network, from chat and commands to shared services.

  5. DumbolEssentials

    Server essentials, our way: commands for players, staff and ranks, plus combat and protection modules.

  6. DumbolDiscord

    Our Discord bot. Tickets for appeals, staff applications, purchases, questions, bugs and player reports, saved as full transcripts. Staff see the player's in-game account right in the ticket, and the bot's status shows how many people are online.

      Toolbox

        Setup

        Arch Linux and Hyprland, with a desktop shell I wrote in Quickshell (QML) called lis. One config covers the bar, launcher, notifications, lock screen and a menu of panels, from a calendar and notes to a task manager and display settings, and it can clean up my disk and update itself. Dark or light, one accent colour, everything on the keyboard. It even dims my other monitors while osu! is focused. Shared as-is in dots-hyprland.
