Dumble Land

The Minecraft server I build

Dumble Land is an Iranian Minecraft server, and a lot of it runs on our own code. Together with The_Dumbledodo I've written most of its plugins: gameplay, economy, the proxy network and the Discord side. The repos are private, so instead of links, here's what each one does.

  • Java 25
  • Paper
  • Velocity
  • Blueprint
  • packetevents
  • Slimefun
  • JDA
  • Gradle

DumbolRolePlay

Private

A whole roleplay gamemode: jobs and professions, gangs, bounties and wanted levels, robberies and prison, ATMs, taxis, medkits and revives, custom items and loot boxes.

432 commits~21k linessince 2025

DumbolExpansion

Private

Our own Slimefun addon: generators, quarries, excavators, capacitors, storage and research, with dozens of custom items and machines.

663 commits~7k linessince 2023

DumbolNetwork

Private

The Velocity proxy plugin and its Paper bridge: the layer that connects every server on the network, from chat and commands to shared services.

313 commits~12k linessince 2023

DumbolGameFramework

Private

The minigame framework behind SuperCrown: arenas, teams and parties, bosses, runes, NPCs, a battle pass and leaderboards.

277 commits~13k lines2024–2025

DumbolEssentials

Private

Server essentials, our way: 60+ commands for players, staff and ranks, plus combat and protection modules.

206 commits~12k linessince 2025

DumbolCore

Private

The survival core from 2023 to 2025: economy, server modules and the glue between Slimefun, ItemsAdder and the rest.

499 commits~9k lines2023–2025

Commit counts are mine; line counts are for the whole project.

LSSH

v0.3.1

Rust1CLI

A simple SSH server manager for the terminal. Save servers once and connect by name, keep passwords in the OS keyring, run commands and copy files across several servers at once, and watch a btop-style live stats bar while you work.

lis, my Hyprland shell
